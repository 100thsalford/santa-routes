ℹ️ How to Use the Map
Using the interactive map:
  • Show/Hide Routes: Click the slide menu icon on the left side of the map to turn routes on/off
  • View Details: Click on any marker to see street names and times
  • Zoom: Pinch to zoom on mobile, scroll wheel on desktop, or use +/- buttons
  • Full Screen: Click the expand button in the top-right to open in Google Maps
  • Navigate: Drag to pan around, or use the search box in the map
💡 Tip: Each route is a different color on the map. Use the slide menu to find your route easily!
