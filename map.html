ℹ️ How to Use Live Tracking
Using the live tracking map:
  • Santa's Position: The red marker shows where Santa is right now
  • Movement Trail: The blue line shows Santa's path over the last 6 hours
  • Click Santa: Click the Santa marker to see detailed information (speed, time, location)
  • Auto-Update: The map refreshes automatically every 30 seconds
  • Zoom & Pan: Use + / - buttons to zoom, or pinch on mobile. Drag to move around
💡 Tip: If Santa appears to be moving slowly or stopped, he might be collecting donations or spreading Christmas cheer! Check the status display above the map.
⏰ Tracking Schedule: Live tracking is only active during Santa's scheduled routes. Check the main page for today's route times.
