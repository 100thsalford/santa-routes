Who We Are

Santa Super Sleigh Salford is run by volunteers from the 100th Salford (St Luke's) Scout Group, part of The Scout Association (a registered charity in England and Wales). We visit the Irlams o' th' Height and Pendlebury neighbourhoods of Salford each December to spread some Christmas cheer and raise funds for the Group.

The times shown on this site for each street are our best estimate, based on the planned route. They aren't guaranteed:

  • We can't guarantee that every street in our catchment will be visited on every planned date, and we don't cover every street in Salford — see the FAQs on our About page for why.

We're not liable for any loss, cost, or disappointment if the sleigh doesn't reach your street when expected, or at all on a given night.

There's no obligation to donate, and donating doesn't entitle you to any particular service, priority, or reward in return.

Meeting the Sleigh Safely

Our sleigh and volunteers operate on public roads and pavements, often in the dark and around traffic, so we ask everyone who comes out to meet us to help keep the visit safe:

  • Please follow the directions of our volunteers and any marshals, and keep a safe distance from the sleigh and its towing vehicle while it's moving.
  • Children should be supervised by a parent or guardian at all times while near the sleigh, the road, or our volunteers.
  • We aren't able to offer rides on the sleigh, or guarantee it will stop for photos on every street — our volunteers will use their judgement on the night, balancing this against the safety of everyone present and the rest of the route still to run.
  • If your child would like to give Santa a letter or card, our volunteers are happy to take it, but we can't guarantee any specific action will be taken as a result, and we won't keep any personal details it contains beyond the night itself.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, we don't accept liability for injury, loss, or damage arising from your attendance at a sleigh visit, except where that liability can't be excluded under English law (for example, death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or fraud).
