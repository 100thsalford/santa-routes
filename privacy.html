Cookie Policy & Privacy Information

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences and provide functionality.

Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Cookie Consent: We store your cookie preference (accept/decline) to remember your choice.

  • Cookie name: santa_sleigh_cookie_consent
  • Duration: 365 days
  • Purpose: Remember your cookie preference

Third-Party Cookies

Facebook Plugins: We embed Facebook content to show updates from our page. Facebook may set cookies for analytics and tracking.

  • Provider: Facebook/Meta
  • Purpose: Social media integration, analytics
  • Privacy Policy: Facebook Privacy Policy

Maps (OpenStreetMap): Our route and live-tracking maps load map tiles from OpenStreetMap's servers. Your browser sends your IP address to those servers to fetch the map images; we don't use Google Maps.

  • Provider: OpenStreetMap Foundation
  • Purpose: Map display
  • Privacy Policy: OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy

Analytics & Usage Data

We use basic, privacy-friendly analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This helps us improve the experience for everyone.

What We Collect

  • Page views: Which pages you visit
  • Button clicks: Which features you use (e.g., "View Map", "Report Sighting")
  • Time on page: How long you spend on our site
Street Search Logging: To help us improve our street search feature and ensure all streets are included, we collect detailed information about searches:
  • Search terms entered: What you typed in the search box
  • Search results: Whether we found a match for your street
  • IP address: Your internet connection's IP address
  • Approximate location: City and region (derived from IP address)
  • Device information: Browser type and operating system

Why We Collect This Data

  • Identify missing streets in our database
  • Understand search patterns to improve the feature
  • Detect and prevent misuse
  • Ensure all Salford neighborhoods are properly covered

What We DON'T Collect

  • Personal information (names, emails, phone numbers)
  • Exact physical addresses
  • Tracking across other websites
  • Browsing history outside our site
  • Financial information

Data Storage & Security

  • Our own records: Street searches and on-site events are stored in our own database, hosted by Netlify
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics for overall visitor numbers. It doesn't use cookies or build profiles of individual visitors
  • No third-party tracking: We don't share data with advertisers or marketing companies
  • Access: Only authorized volunteers can access analytics data

Santa Sighting Reports

When you submit a Santa sighting report, we collect:

  • Street name (selected from our routes)
  • Time of sighting
  • Timestamp of report submission

This information is stored in Google Forms and displayed publicly on our website to help others track Santa's progress. We do not collect any personal information (names, emails, addresses) with sighting reports.

Address and Postcode Searches

If you search for an address or postcode, the text you type is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service and/or postcodes.io so we can work out which street you mean and find the nearest street Santa will visit. We don't send your name or any other details with it.

Donations

Donations are taken through Square's secure checkout, on Square's own pages. Square collects and processes your payment and contact details under its own privacy policy; we never see your card details. We receive only the fundraising totals.

Volunteers: What We Collect and Why

If you help with the Santa Super Sleigh as a volunteer (by invitation, by registering with a sign-up code, or because a leader has added you to a rota), we hold the following information about you:

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Your password is handled by our login provider (Netlify Identity) and is never visible to us.
  • Contact details (optional): a mobile number, and a preferred email address for reminders if you give one. If a leader adds you as a helper without a login, they may record your name and a phone number or email so they can contact you.
  • Rota information: the nights and routes you've signed up for or been assigned to, and any role you're qualified or assigned for (Santa, safety walker, driver).
  • Safety acknowledgment: the date and time you confirmed you'd read our safety guidance.
  • Reminder preference: whether you want to receive reminder emails.
  • Registration records: when you registered, whether your registration was approved, and a short-term record of the IP address used for sign-up attempts, which we use only to stop abuse of the sign-up form.

Why we use it:

  • To run the volunteer rota and tell you where and when to be on the night
  • To send shift reminders and occasional updates about the event you've volunteered for
  • To keep volunteers, residents and children safe, including making sure everyone has seen our safety guidance
  • To contact you if plans change on the night
  • To check that registrations come from people we've asked to help

Who can see it: Group leaders and site administrators can see volunteer records. Other logged-in volunteers can see the names (not email addresses or phone numbers) of the people signed up to the same nights. We don't use volunteer details for marketing, and we don't share them with anyone outside the services listed under Data Sharing below.

How long we keep it: We keep volunteer details while you remain an active volunteer with the Group and remove them on request or when they're no longer needed for running the event. You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time using the contact details below.

AI-Generated Content

Some of the graphics on this site (such as the seasonal holding-page artwork) were created using AI image-generation tools rather than drawn or photographed by hand.

Legal Basis for Data Processing

Under UK GDPR, we process your data based on:

  • Legitimate interests: Improving our website and ensuring comprehensive route coverage for the community benefit
  • Volunteers: We process volunteer information because it's necessary for the legitimate interests of running the event safely and effectively, and so that we can carry out the volunteering arrangement you've agreed to with the Group
  • Consent: By using the search feature, you consent to this logging. You can choose not to use the search feature if you don't want this data collected

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access: Know what data we collect about you
  • Rectification: Request corrections to your data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Objection: Object to data processing
  • Data portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Lodge a complaint: Contact the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office) if you have concerns

Managing Cookies & Privacy

You can manage cookies and your privacy in several ways:

  • Cookie banner: Click "Decline" to reject non-essential cookies
  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies
  • Don't use search: Browse street lists manually to avoid search logging
  • VPN/Proxy: Use a VPN to mask your IP address
  • Private browsing: Use incognito/private mode

Note: If you decline cookies, Facebook updates and interactive maps may not display properly.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We use the following services to run the site, and they handle data on our behalf or under their own privacy policies:

  • Netlify: Hosts the website and runs our database (including search and analytics records and volunteer information), file storage (route maps) and volunteer login. Netlify privacy policy
  • Resend: Sends our emails to volunteers (such as registration notices, invites and shift reminders), so your name, email address and the email content pass through it. Resend privacy policy
  • Cloudflare: Provides cookie-free website visitor statistics. Cloudflare privacy policy
  • Square: Takes online donations. Square privacy policy
  • Google (Forms and Sheets): Stores the street name and time of Santa sighting reports. No personal details are collected.
  • OpenStreetMap and postcodes.io: Receive the addresses and postcodes you type into the street search (see above), and OpenStreetMap supplies our map images.
  • Facebook (Meta): Provides the embedded updates from our Facebook page.
  • TruTrak: Supplies the sleigh's GPS location for the live tracker. This is the vehicle's position only, not personal data about visitors.
  • Law enforcement: If legally required

Search data may be reviewed by authorized Scout volunteers to identify missing streets and improve coverage.

Children's Privacy

Our website is family-friendly and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents should supervise their children's internet use.

Contact Us

If you have questions about our privacy practices, want to exercise your rights, or request data deletion, please contact us:

  • Facebook: Santa Super Sleigh Salford
